# Accounts with a buying group active

## Step 1: Build the multi-stakeholder list

Build a segment of accounts named "Multi-Stakeholder Engaged Accounts". An account is in the segment only when all of these are true: the users in the account together did the Session start event 5 or more times in the last 14 days, the users in the account together did the View page event 10 or more times in the last 14 days.

## Step 2: Alert sales

Generate a Slack message to the sales channel listing the accounts in "Multi-Stakeholder Engaged Accounts" so reps know a buying committee may have formed and can act while interest is high. Use the result of "Build the multi-stakeholder list".

## Step 3: Send a team resource

Generate a designed marketing email for the accounts in "Multi-Stakeholder Engaged Accounts" offering a resource built for teams evaluating the product together. Three sentences, consultative tone, no discount language, one button linking to a call-booking page. Use the result of "Build the multi-stakeholder list".
