# Free users researching heavily

## Step 1: Build the high-intent list

Build a segment of users named "High-Intent Visitors". A user is in the segment only when all of these are true: their Plan attribute is "free", they did the View page event 3 or more times in the last 14 days.

## Step 2: Nudge toward a paid feature

Generate a push notification for the users in "High-Intent Visitors" highlighting one feature only available on a paid plan. Title under 40 characters, one short body line, helpful and not pushy tone, deep link to the pricing page. Use the result of "Build the high-intent list".

## Step 3: Share the shortlist with growth

Generate a plain-text email to the growth team distribution list listing the users in "High-Intent Visitors" so outreach can be timed to their actual research activity. Use the result of "Build the high-intent list".
